Spaced repetition is a simple idea: **review material just before you would forget it**.

## The core loop

1. Learn something once
2. Review after a short delay
3. If you recall it, wait longer next time
4. If you forget, review sooner

## Why it works

Memory fades on a curve. Short, timed reviews flatten that curve better than cramming.

## How to use it here

- Put notes under a shared `topic`
- Open **Learn → Review** for that topic
- Cycle through cards and open full notes when you need detail

You do not need a fancy algorithm to benefit — consistent, spaced reviews already help.
